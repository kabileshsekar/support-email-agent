# User Guide and Troubleshooting: Nimbus Slate 10

## Tablet will not turn on
1. Charge with the included 18 W charger for at least 30 minutes.
2. Hold the power button for 15 seconds to force a restart.
3. If the screen stays black but the tablet vibrates, hold Power + Volume Up for 10 seconds.

## Tablet is slow or apps crash
Check for system updates in Settings > System > Update. Keep at least 10% of storage free. Clear the cache of the affected app in Settings > Apps.

## Battery drains quickly
Lower the refresh rate from 90 Hz to 60 Hz in Settings > Display and reduce brightness. Video playback lasts up to 12 hours on a full charge.

## Touch screen is unresponsive
Remove any screen protector that is not designed for the Slate 10, clean the screen, and restart. If parts of the screen never respond, contact support with the serial number.

## Expanding storage
The Slate 10 accepts microSD cards up to 1 TB. Format the card as portable storage in Settings > Storage.
