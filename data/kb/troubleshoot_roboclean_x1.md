# User Guide and Troubleshooting: Nimbus RoboClean X1

## First run
Charge the vacuum fully on its dock (about 4 hours) before the first clean. Open the Nimbus app to create a map; the first mapping run takes longer than normal cleans.

## Error codes
- E1: Wheel stuck. Lift the robot and check the wheels for hair or debris.
- E2: Main brush jammed. Remove the brush cover and clean hair from the brush.
- E3: Cliff sensors dirty. Wipe the sensors under the front bumper with a dry cloth.
- E4: Low battery and cannot find the dock. Place the robot on the dock manually.
- E5: LiDAR blocked. Make sure nothing is pressing on the top sensor and remove any stickers.

## Vacuum is not charging
Clean the charging contacts on the robot and the dock with a dry cloth. Make sure the dock is on a flat surface against a wall with 0.5 m of clear space on each side and 1.5 m in front. If the battery light does not come on after 10 minutes on the dock, restart the robot by holding the power button for 10 seconds.

## Weak suction or poor cleaning
Empty the 0.4 L dustbin, clean the filter every week, and replace it every 3 months. Check the side brushes for tangled hair.

## Map lost or robot gets lost
Update to firmware 4.2.0 or later, which fixed map-loss issues after power cuts. Keep the dock in the same place, and avoid moving it after mapping.

## Mopping
Fill the 250 ml water tank with clean water only; cleaning chemicals can damage the pump and are not covered by warranty.
