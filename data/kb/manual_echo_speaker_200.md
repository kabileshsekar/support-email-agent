# User Manual: Nimbus Echo Speaker 200

## Setting up the speaker
1. Plug the speaker into power. The light ring pulses blue when it is ready to set up.
2. Open the Nimbus app, tap "Add device" and choose Echo Speaker 200.
3. Select your Wi-Fi network and enter the password. The speaker supports 2.4 GHz and 5 GHz networks.
4. The light ring turns solid white once setup is complete.

## Light ring meanings
- Pulsing blue: ready for setup.
- Solid white: connected and idle.
- Pulsing orange: no Wi-Fi connection.
- Solid red: microphones muted.
- Pulsing green: firmware update in progress (do not unplug).

## Multi-room audio
Group up to 8 Nimbus speakers in the app under Rooms > Create group.

## Updating the firmware
Updates install automatically overnight. To update manually, open the app, select the speaker and choose Settings > Firmware > Check for updates.

## Factory reset
Press and hold the Volume Down and Mute buttons together for 15 seconds until the light ring flashes orange, then set the speaker up again in the app.
