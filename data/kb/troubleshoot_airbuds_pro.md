# User Guide and Troubleshooting: Nimbus AirBuds Pro

## Pairing for the first time
Open the charging case near your phone with the buds inside. Press and hold the case button for 3 seconds until the light flashes white, then select "Nimbus AirBuds Pro" in your phone's Bluetooth settings.

## Connecting to two devices (multipoint)
Pair the buds with the second device using the same steps. Both devices stay connected and audio switches automatically.

## Only one earbud is playing
1. Put both buds in the case, close the lid for 10 seconds, then open it again.
2. Clean the charging contacts on the buds and inside the case with a dry cotton swab.
3. Forget the buds in your phone's Bluetooth settings and reset them: with the buds in the open case, hold the case button for 15 seconds until the light flashes amber, then pair again.

## Case or buds will not charge
Use a USB-C cable and a 5 V adapter, or a Qi charging pad. The case light shows amber while charging and green when full. If the case does not light up, leave it charging for 30 minutes before trying again. Charging contacts must be clean and dry.

## Poor noise cancelling or sound quality
Try a different ear tip size for a better seal (three sizes are included). Update the firmware in the Nimbus app; firmware 1.8 improved ANC performance.

## Battery life is shorter than expected
Battery life is 8 hours with ANC off and 6 hours with ANC on. LDAC codec and high volume also reduce battery life. Normal battery wear is not covered by warranty, but a sudden drop to under 2 hours may indicate a fault.
