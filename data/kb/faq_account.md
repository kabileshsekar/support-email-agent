# FAQ: Account, App and Payments

## Resetting your password
Go to the sign-in page and select "Forgot password". We email a reset link that is valid for 30 minutes. If you do not receive it, check your spam folder. Support staff will never ask for your password.

## Changing your account email
Open the Nimbus app or website, go to Account > Profile and choose "Change email". You will need to confirm the change from both the old and the new address.

## Deleting your account
You can request account deletion from Account > Privacy > Delete account. Deletion is completed within 30 days, as required by privacy law. Devices registered to the account will be unlinked.

## The Nimbus app
The Nimbus app (iOS 15+ and Android 9+) is used to set up devices, install firmware updates, and view warranty information. Sign in with your Nimbus account.

## Payment methods
We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, PayPal and Apple Pay. Payment is taken when the order is placed. We never ask for card details by email.

## Contact hours
Email support replies 7 days a week. Phone support is available Monday to Friday, 8 am to 8 pm Eastern Time, at 1-800-555-0199.

## Membership tiers
Customers have a membership tier (standard, gold or platinum) based on purchases. Gold members get free express shipping; platinum members also get a dedicated support specialist.
