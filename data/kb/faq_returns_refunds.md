# FAQ: Returns, Refunds and Cancellations

## Return window
You can return any product within 30 days of delivery for a full refund, as long as it is in its original packaging with all accessories. Defective products can be returned within the same 30 days; after 30 days defective products are handled under warranty.

## How to start a return
Log in to your Nimbus account, open Orders, choose the order and select "Return item". You will receive a prepaid return label by email. Return shipping is free for defective items; for change-of-mind returns a $6.95 return shipping fee is deducted from the refund.

## Refund timing
Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5-7 business days after the return arrives at our warehouse. Your bank may take an additional 2-3 business days to show the credit.

## Cancelling an order
Orders can be cancelled free of charge while their status is processing, from the Orders page or by asking support. Once an order has shipped it can no longer be cancelled; please refuse the delivery or start a return when it arrives.

## Non-returnable items
Opened earbud ear tips, gift cards and items damaged by misuse cannot be returned.

## Refund exceptions and compensation
Requests for refunds outside the 30-day window, partial refunds, price adjustments or compensation are reviewed individually by the customer care team. Support agents cannot approve these automatically; the request is passed to a specialist who replies within 2 business days.

## Price match
We match the price of our own website if the price drops within 14 days of your purchase. Contact support with your order number.
