# User Guide and Troubleshooting: Nimbus Pulse Watch 3

## Pairing the watch
Install the Nimbus app, turn on Bluetooth, and tap "Add device" > Pulse Watch 3. Keep the watch on its charger during first setup; a firmware update usually installs automatically.

## Battery drains quickly
Typical battery life is up to 5 days. The following features reduce battery life: always-on display, continuous SpO2 measurement, GPS workouts and high screen brightness. Firmware 2.1.0 fixed a battery-drain bug affecting versions 2.0.x, so update the watch first: Nimbus app > Device > Firmware.

## Watch is not syncing with the phone
1. Make sure the Nimbus app is allowed to run in the background and has Bluetooth permission.
2. Restart the phone and the watch (hold the side button for 10 seconds).
3. In the app, pull down on the dashboard to force a sync.
4. If sync still fails, unpair the watch in the app and pair it again. Health data stored in the cloud is kept.

## Heart rate readings look wrong
Wear the watch a finger's width above the wrist bone, snug but comfortable. Clean the sensor on the back. Readings during high-intensity workouts can lag by a few seconds.

## Watch will not charge
Make sure the magnetic charger is aligned with the pins on the back and that the pins are clean. Use a 5 V USB adapter. If no charging icon appears after 30 minutes, contact support.

## Water resistance
The watch is rated 5 ATM: suitable for swimming and showering, not for diving, saunas or high-speed water sports. Water damage beyond the rating is not covered by warranty.
