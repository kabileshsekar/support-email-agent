# FAQ: Shipping and Delivery

## Shipping options and times
Nimbus Electronics offers two shipping methods. Standard shipping takes 3-5 business days after the order ships and is free on orders over $50. Express shipping takes 1-2 business days and costs $14.99. Orders placed before 2 pm local time on a business day usually leave the warehouse the same day.

## Order statuses explained
- processing: payment is confirmed and the warehouse is picking and packing the order. The order can still be cancelled or the address changed.
- shipped: the parcel has been handed to the carrier. A tracking number is emailed and is visible in your account.
- out_for_delivery: the carrier expects to deliver today.
- delivered: the carrier has marked the parcel as delivered.
- delayed: the carrier has reported a delay. A new estimated delivery date is shown when the carrier provides one.
- cancelled: the order was cancelled and any payment refunded to the original payment method.
- returned: the return was received at our warehouse and the refund is being processed.

## Tracking a parcel
Use the tracking number from your shipping confirmation email on the carrier's website (UPS, FedEx or DHL). Tracking information can take up to 24 hours to appear after the order ships.

## Delayed or missing parcels
If your order is marked delayed, the carrier has a new estimated delivery date and no action is needed. If tracking shows no movement for 7 days after the estimated delivery date, or the parcel is marked delivered but you cannot find it, contact support: we will open a carrier investigation, which takes up to 5 business days, and then reship or refund the order.

## Changing the delivery address
The delivery address can only be changed while the order status is processing. After the order has shipped, you can ask the carrier to redirect the parcel using the tracking link.

## International shipping
We currently ship to the United States and Canada only. Canadian orders may be subject to import duties collected by the carrier.
