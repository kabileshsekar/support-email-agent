# FAQ: Warranty and NimbusCare+

## Standard warranty
Every Nimbus product comes with a limited warranty starting on the purchase date: 12 months for speakers, earbuds, smartwatches, tablets and cameras, and 24 months for routers and robot vacuums. The warranty covers manufacturing defects and hardware failures under normal use.

## What is not covered
The standard warranty does not cover accidental damage (drops, cracked screens), liquid damage beyond the product's water-resistance rating, normal battery wear, cosmetic wear, loss or theft, or damage caused by unauthorised repairs.

## NimbusCare+ extended protection
NimbusCare+ adds 24 months to the standard warranty, includes one accidental-damage claim, and gives priority replacement shipping. It can be bought within 60 days of the product purchase.

## How to make a warranty claim
1. Make sure the device is on the latest firmware and try the troubleshooting guide for your product.
2. Contact support with the device serial number (found in the Nimbus app under Device > About, or on the label underneath the device).
3. Support confirms warranty eligibility and opens a claim. The warranty team reviews the claim and, if approved, sends a prepaid shipping label for repair or replacement.
Warranty claims, repairs and replacements are approved by the warranty team, not by front-line support. Approved replacements ship within 3 business days.

## Out-of-warranty devices
If your device is outside its warranty, we offer an out-of-warranty repair service at a flat fee depending on the product, or a 20% loyalty discount on a replacement product. Contact support to get a quote.

## Finding your serial number
Serial numbers start with "SN-" and are printed on the device label, the original box, and in the Nimbus app under Device > About.
