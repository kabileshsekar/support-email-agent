# Troubleshooting: Nimbus Echo Speaker 200

## Speaker will not connect to Wi-Fi (pulsing orange light)
1. Restart your router and the speaker (unplug for 30 seconds).
2. Make sure your phone is on the same Wi-Fi network during setup and Bluetooth is enabled on your phone.
3. If your router uses a combined 2.4/5 GHz network name, temporarily disable band steering or try the 2.4 GHz band.
4. Move the speaker within 10 metres of the router.
5. If it still fails, factory reset the speaker (hold Volume Down + Mute for 15 seconds) and set it up again.

## Speaker keeps disconnecting
Disconnections are most often fixed by updating to the latest firmware (3.4.0 or later), which improved Wi-Fi stability. Also assign the speaker a fixed IP address in your router if possible.

## No sound or distorted sound
Check that the speaker is not muted (solid red ring) and the volume is up. Remove the speaker from any multi-room group and test it alone. Distortion at high volume on firmware older than 3.3 is fixed by updating.

## Voice assistant does not respond
Make sure the microphones are not muted, and that the voice assistant is linked in the app under Settings > Voice.

## When to contact support
If the speaker does not power on with a known-good outlet, or the light ring stays solid red after unmuting, the speaker may be faulty. Contact support with the serial number to check warranty.
