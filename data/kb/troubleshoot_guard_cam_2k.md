# User Guide and Troubleshooting: Nimbus Guard Cam 2K

## Setting up the camera
The Guard Cam 2K connects to 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi only. In the Nimbus app tap Add device > Guard Cam 2K and scan the QR code shown in the app with the camera.

## Camera shows offline
1. Check the camera has power: the status light should be solid blue.
2. Make sure your router's 2.4 GHz band is enabled; the camera cannot join 5 GHz networks.
3. Move the camera closer to the router or add a mesh node; outdoor cameras often have weak signal through walls.
4. Update to firmware 2.6 or later, which fixed reconnection after router restarts.
5. Remove and re-add the camera in the app.

## Night vision is blurry
Clean the lens and make sure no window glass is in front of the camera; infrared reflects off glass. Night vision reaches up to 10 m.

## Recording storage
Recordings are saved to a microSD card up to 256 GB or to Nimbus Cloud. Format new cards in the app under Camera > Storage.

## Weatherproofing
The camera is rated IP65 and can be mounted outdoors. Keep the USB connector covered with the supplied rubber cap.
